About Us

We started in 2018 with a small food truck, a secret family dry rub, and one beat-up deep fryer. Our goal was simple: serve crispy, juicy, flavor-packed fried chicken that people dream about. Today, our kitchen is bigger, but our founding dream remains exactly the same.

Great chicken takes patience. We source fresh, local poultry daily. Every single piece is marinated for 24 hours in our signature buttermilk brine, hand-dredged in our proprietary spice blend, and fried fresh to order. No shortcuts, ever.

We believe local tastes better. We partner with nearby independent farmers and growers who raise poultry without unnecessary additives. Even our crisp pickles and signature dipping sauces are made completely from scratch every morning.

We are a crew of food lovers, hospitality nerds, and spice fanatics. When you walk through our doors, you aren't just a customer; you're part of the coop. We strive to make every visit feel like a backyard Sunday cookout.
